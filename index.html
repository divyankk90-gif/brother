The Unwritten Manifesto of Brotherhood

Kyunki tu sirf dost nahi, meri poori zindagi ka sabse anmol hissa hai.

Mere Bhai,

Aaj ke is khaas din par, mai dil se tujhe batana chahta hoon ki teri haseen presence meri life me kya maane rakhti hai. Duniya me dost toh bohot milte hain, par ek aisa shaksh milna jo aapko aapki khamiyon ke sath accept kare, jo aapke bina kahe aapki har khushi aur har pareshani samajh le—wo naseeb sirf khushkismat logon ko milta hai. Aur mai wo khushkismat insaan hoon jise tere jaisa bhai mila.

Hum dono ka rishta kisi khoon ke rishte se kam nahi hai. Humne sath me haseen pal bitaye hain, bewajah ki baaton par thahake lagaye hain, aur life ke mushkil waqt me ek dusre ka hath thama hai. Jab bhi mujhe laga ki mai akele pad raha hoon, tera bas ek hi dialogue hota tha—"Bhai tu chinta mat kar, mai hoon na." Wo chaar shabd mere liye duniya ki sabse badi taqat ban jate hain.

Tera dil itna saaf (pure-hearted) hai ki aaj ke is swarthi zamane me teri jaisa bacha-kucha sacha insaan dekh kar sukoon milta hai. Tu kabhi kisi ka bura nahi sochta, sabki madad ke liye hamesha taiyar rehta hai. Teri yahi saadgi aur amiri teri pehchan hai jo tujhe baakiyon se alag aur behad qeemti banati hai.

Mai dua karta hoon ki tera yeh naya saal tere liye beshumar khushiyan, sacha sukoon, aur kamyabi ke naye raste khole. Tu jo bhi sapna dekhe, wo haqeeqat ban jaye. Tera bhai hamesha, har mod par, tere sath khada rahega—chahe halat kaise bhi hon.

Tera Bhai, Hamesha Ke Liye.
